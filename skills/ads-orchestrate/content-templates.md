# Social Content Templates (brand-consistent)

Used by `ads-orchestrate` to turn trend topics + `brand-profile.json` into
platform-native social drafts. Voice and palette come from the brand profile;
the trend supplies the hook. Always pass the FEEL gate before publishing.

## Cadence (default, e-commerce / perfume)

| Platform | Frequency | Primary format | Goal |
|----------|-----------|----------------|------|
| Instagram | 1/day | Reel 9:16 + carousel | Discovery + brand |
| TikTok | 3–5/week | Sound-on Reel 9:16 | Trend reach |
| Facebook | 3/week | Image/carousel | Retargeting |

## Post template

```
HOOK: {trend topic, on-brand}
BODY: 1–2 sentences in {brand voice}; tie product to the trend
CTA: {primary CTA}
HASHTAGS: 3–5 niche + 2 trend
VISUAL: {brand palette}; mode from /ads photoshoot
```

## Rules

- TikTok: sound-on only (mirrors Quality Gates in `ads/SKILL.md`).
- IG carousel: hook in slide 1; product proof by slide 3.
- One concept = caption variants per platform; never cross-post verbatim.
- Trend must be brand-safe (no tragedy/controversy hijacking).
